# Client workflow (repeatable)

1. **Inquiry** – reply within a day, ask for a short call.
2. **Requirements** – use the questions below.
3. **Specification** – copy `PROJECT_BRIEF.md`, fill in the client's facts.
4. **Scope & deliverables** – pages, languages, who writes/proofreads text, number of change rounds.
5. **Price & schedule** – written; separate one-off work from running costs.
6. **Build** – replace `data.json`, template text, translations; `python3 build.py`.
7. **Test** – automated checks + manual table in TESTING_CHECKLIST.
8. **Approval** – client reviews every language in writing.
9. **Publish** – DEPLOYMENT_GUIDE.
10. **Handover** – source folder, CUSTOMIZATION_GUIDE, account ownership with the client.
11. **Maintenance** – explain domain, hosting, form service renewals and who changes what.

## Questions for the client
Business name, legal form and address; services and prices; opening hours; languages and who proofreads; existing logo/colours/photos and their rights; how requests should reach them (e-mail, phone, booking tool); existing domain; who owns accounts; need for Impressum/privacy texts (their lawyer or generator); deadline; budget.

## Needs a human or a professional
Legal pages and data-protection decisions (lawyer/data-protection officer); native-speaker proofreading; photo licences; final approval.
